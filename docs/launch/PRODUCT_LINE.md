## Design 1: Core Logo Hoodie
**Concept:** Bold, minimalist +++ logo front center
**Placement:** Chest (6" width) + Small back neck logo (2" width)
**Style:** Clean, recognizable, foundational brand piece

### Colorways (5)
1. **Black** - Classic foundation (highest volume)
2. **White** - Clean contrast (second highest)
3. **Sport Grey** - Neutral everyday wear
4. **Navy** - Premium alternative to black
5. **Forest Green** - Differentiator, earth tone appeal
